VoxCPM2 text-to-speech, running entirely on this device.

Requires Ponko 2.0.0 or newer (plugin API 12).

Model weights are NOT bundled with this APK. Download them in the plugin
(Model tab) or import your own two GGUF files. The weights keep their own
licence: the official VoxCPM2 weights are Apache-2.0; the Lite Q4 build is a
third-party conversion whose repository declares no licence.

Pure CPU inference. Expect roughly 1-2 minutes for 5 seconds of speech.
8 GB of RAM is recommended.
